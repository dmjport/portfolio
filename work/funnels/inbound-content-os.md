# Inbound Content OS

**Context:** Built and ran the inbound engine as Head of Growth at a cold-calling coaching SaaS, and drove organic for two other companies (a fintech media platform and a trading-education company).

## Objective
Pull buyers in before they ever talk to sales, and make content a compounding asset rather than a campaign that resets every quarter.

## What I shipped
- A daily content engine: source from calls and conversations, then clips and post drafts, then cross-platform distribution, then a landing page and form, then enrichment, then a real-time Slack alert, then email nurture.
- A 370+ day consecutive publishing streak, instrumented end to end.
- Two independent SEO programs, one editorial, one programmatic.

## Outcome
- 754 LinkedIn posts at 2.58M impressions.
- Editorial SEO: 0 to 1.78M monthly organic visits, zero paid, 245+ articles published.
- Programmatic SEO: +599% clicks (33K to 237K) across 21K+ indexed pages.
- YouTube: 49,703 views from iterating into content-market fit.

## Stack
LinkedIn, YouTube, X, GA4 and Search Console (attribution), Clay and Slack (enrichment and routing), Claude (drafting), launchd (daily scheduling).
