# Cold Calling

**Context:** Ran outbound calling programs across multiple B2B client engagements, plus my own.

## Objective
Generate activated, qualified leads and booked meetings from cold lists, and make the results measurable enough to improve every cycle.

## What I shipped
- A completions-based framework (measure conversations completed, not dials thrown) with disposition analytics on every call.
- P1 list scoring that ranked prospects before reps ever dialed.
- Vertical testing to find where the message actually landed.
- A dialer-agnostic call pipeline with auto-sync to Google Sheets for reporting.

## Outcome
- 850+ completed conversations across engagements.
- 60+ meetings booked.
- P1-scored leads converted roughly 5x better than unscored.
- Tight verticals crushed generic targeting: a restaurant vertical hit 14.3% versus about 0.1% for generic B2B SaaS.
- ~$1.43M in identified pipeline from activated leads.

## Stack
Salesfinity and Frontspin (dialers), Python and SQLite (scoring and disposition analytics), Google Sheets (reporting), Whisper and Claude (call analysis).
