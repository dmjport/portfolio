# Job-Application Pipeline

**Context:** A personal automation that runs my own job search like a GTM campaign.

## Objective
Apply the same pipeline discipline I use for sales to my own job search: source, enrich, personalize, and track at volume.

## What I shipped
- A daily scheduled pipeline: discover roles from multiple sources, then enrich (hiring manager and contact lookup), then generate a tailored resume, cover note, and outreach message, then queue (email draft and LinkedIn DM), then track through status states.

## Outcome
- A self-running top of funnel for the job search, with tailored materials generated per role instead of one generic resume sprayed everywhere.

## Stack
Python, multiple job-board sources, enrichment APIs, Claude (tailoring), Gmail drafts, launchd (daily, 7:30 AM).
